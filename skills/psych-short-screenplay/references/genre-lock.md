# Genre Lock

Pick one engine. Do not blend by accident.

| Engine | Audience feeling | Must happen | Ending |
|---|---|---|---|
| Psychological horror | Dread, doubt | Perception is unsafe | Ambiguity is valid |
| Psychological thriller | Tension, urgency | A hidden truth is pursued | A reveal is expected |
| Hybrid | Dread that tightens | A reveal arrives; the world stays unsafe | Reveal + leftover contamination |

Default for "psychological thriller horror" = **hybrid**.

## Hard rules

- Threat hooks the protagonist's false belief.
- Antagonist is a person, system, memory, or the protagonist. Supernatural is pressure, not the plot.
- Withholding beats showing.
- If a scare still works after you explain it, it is not a scare. Cut it.

## Short-film compression

No B-story romance. No origin dump. No slasher kill ladder. One procedure gets corrupted: logbook, lockup, bedtime, intake form, shift change, prayer, factory checklist.

Isolation is earned. Phones fail because of a planted rule or a choice, not because Act Two needed it.
